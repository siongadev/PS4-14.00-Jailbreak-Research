# Font loading lead (separate from streams)

**Status, 2026-10-19:** A one-shot adaptation of WebKit's published font-loading regression triggered an immediate system-memory warning and page refresh on the operator's 14.00 PS4. Two distinct controls completed normally. This is a strong, specific browser-failure lead, not a demonstrated native read/write primitive or a proven use-after-free in the running binary.

WebKit's [CVE-2026-43715 advisory](https://www.webkitgtk.org/security/WSA-2026-0004.html) describes a use-after-free in malicious web content. Its [regression test and callback-iteration change](https://github.com/WebKit/WebKit/commit/5aedb82710ba578f61501106e78f6b25b7f7a558) identify reentrant `CSSFontFace` client notification: a `FontFace.prototype.then` getter removes a stylesheet while `face.load()` is resolving the loaded promise. The [upstream `FontFace.cpp` at that commit](https://github.com/WebKit/WebKit/blob/5aedb82710ba578f61501106e78f6b25b7f7a558/Source/WebCore/css/FontFace.cpp#L1579-L1587) uses `protect(m_backing)->load()`. WebKit's [GLib backport note](https://github.com/WebKit/WebKit/commit/f1fff08a99d77bac49c2500ece3fdcfac06c8f1c) distinguishes that memory-safety fix from the later callback-iteration guard, which prevents a debug-only `hasFace()` assertion. That advisory covers WebKitGTK/WPE, not this console.

The Sony WebKit archive [labeled `13.00 -`](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/oss/ps4/webkit/) contains an unguarded `iterateClients()` in `Source/WebCore/css/CSSFontFace.cpp` (lines 55–59). `setStatus()` calls it at lines 591–593 and then continues using the face. The same archive's `FontFace.cpp` has `loadForBindings()` call `m_backing->load()` without a local retaining reference (lines 358–363). `CSSFontFaceSet::fontStateChanged()` has `ASSERT(hasFace(face))` at lines 524–527. These are source observations, not proof of the 14.00 binary's contents. Exact member hashes are recorded in [font source provenance](../references/FONT_FACE_SOURCE.md).

The earlier `surface_inventory_v1` capture `5de5a37bb2f54475840a96e8876f2244` reports `FontFace` exposed. All four valid tests used the 14.00 user-agent form and the operator's Settings-confirmed 14.00 console. The reentrant and removal-control commands used separate fresh sessions after the warning refreshed the page. Their exact source and manifest bytes are in the local probe snapshot store. The markers show that the reentrant getter and layout ran; they do not identify the faulting instruction. The additional marked-getter control excludes the synchronous milestone call by itself as the reason the failing run stopped reporting.

Both controls that removed the stylesheet **after** `face.load()` returned reported `face.status` as `unloaded`; the non-removal Helvetica control reported `loaded`. That is consistent with style recalculation changing the wrapper's backing face (`FontFace::adopt()` appears at lines 325–329 of the Sony source) and explains why an unprotected `m_backing->load()` is concerning when the same change occurs before it returns. This is a source-backed interpretation of browser-visible status, not a direct observation of native object lifetime.

The matched upstream test, vulnerable-looking Sony source, positive callback control, and passing non-reentrant style-removal control make this a stronger lead than a bare warning. Still, the browser warning may represent a fail-stop path. A native crash signature or controlled userland-memory effect is needed to establish the specific native failure.
